YOUR SAFETY MATTERS

Trust & Safety

IMI DIGITAL HUB shares job vacancies, scholarships, internships, grants and training opportunities to help people discover useful leads. Please read this guidance before contacting an organisation or submitting an application.

Important: A listing on this portal is not a guarantee of employment, admission, funding or selection. You are responsible for confirming the opportunity and its requirements directly with the organisation using its official website or verified contact channels.

How we review submissions

  • Visitor-submitted opportunities are held for review and are not published automatically.
  • Our team checks the information provided, including the organisation, description and official source link, before deciding whether to publish.
  • We may edit a listing for clarity, request corrections, reject it or remove it if it appears misleading, outdated or unsafe.
  • Review does not mean that we are affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting as an agent for the organisation.

Protect yourself from scams

  • Do not pay an individual or unofficial agent to secure a job, scholarship, grant, interview or guaranteed selection.
  • Be cautious of requests for application, processing, registration, training or equipment fees. Confirm any legitimate fee directly on the organisation's official website before paying.
  • Never share your password, one-time password (OTP), ATM PIN, card PIN or banking login details with a recruiter or contributor.
  • Do not send sensitive identity or financial documents until you have independently confirmed who is requesting them and why.
  • Check website addresses carefully. Scammers may imitate the names, logos and email addresses of genuine organisations.
  • Be wary of unrealistic salaries, guaranteed awards, urgent pressure to pay, or requests to move the conversation to suspicious private accounts.

Before you apply

  1. Open the official source link and confirm that the opportunity is still open.
  2. Check the organisation's own careers, admissions or programme page for eligibility, deadlines and application instructions.
  3. Use contact details published on the organisation's official website if you need clarification.
  4. Keep a copy of your application and any official confirmation you receive.

Report a suspicious or inaccurate listing

If you notice a listing that appears fraudulent, misleading, expired or incorrect, please send us the opportunity title, its portal link and a short explanation. Do not include passwords, OTPs or other sensitive personal information in your report.
