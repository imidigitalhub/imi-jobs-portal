ABOUT THIS PLATFORM

Opportunity information you can act on.

IMI DIGITAL HUB helps job seekers, students, graduates and professionals discover opportunities in Nigeria and beyond. Explore employment opportunities, graduate and entry-level jobs, remote roles, scholarships, internships, fellowships, grants, skills-development courses and professional training programmes. Each listing is provided to help you find useful leads and the relevant application information in one place.

Use the search and category filters to explore opportunities that match your interests. Before applying, confirm eligibility, deadlines, fees and application instructions directly on the official organisation's website. Never pay an unverified person for a guaranteed job, scholarship or award.
